## Summary

This document discusses the differentiation of anterior ST-elevation myocardial infarction (STEMI) from early repolarization using specific electrocardiographic (ECG) characteristics. A retrospective cohort study was conducted in two urban hospitals, involving patients with confirmed acute LAD occlusion and those with non-ischemic chest pain. Key diagnostic variables include ST-segment elevation, R-wave amplitude, and QTc interval, with a derived ECG equation showing high sensitivity and specificity for distinguishing subtle STEMI. The study highlights the importance of these ECG criteria, though it notes limitations such as retrospective design and complex calculations. Clinicians are advised to use these findings in conjunction with comprehensive patient assessment, supported by online tools for practical application.

## Clinical Point

Are there specific ECG characteristics that can differentiate between anterior ST-elevation myocardial infarction (STEMI) and early repolarization?

## Bottom Line

This study derived and validated an ECG score to help differentiate anterior ST elevation from early repolarization. Online apps and calculators are available to assist but should be used by clinicians who understand the score in the context of the whole patient presentation.
