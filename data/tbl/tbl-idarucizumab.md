## Summary

Idarucizumab is evaluated for its efficacy and safety in reversing dabigatran anticoagulation in patients with serious bleeding or requiring urgent surgery. This prospective cohort study, conducted across 184 sites in 35 countries, involved 90 patients divided into two groups: those with life-threatening bleeding and those needing urgent procedures. The intervention involved administering 5g of idarucizumab intravenously, resulting in a 100% median reversal of anticoagulation as measured by dilute thrombin and ecarin-clotting times. Clotting tests normalized in the majority of patients, with a significant reduction in unbound dabigatran levels. Despite a small sample size and lack of a control group, the study suggests idarucizumab effectively reverses dabigatran effects, though further research is needed to fully assess its safety profile.

## Clinical Point

In patients taking dabigatran who present with serious bleeding or require urgent surgery/intervention, is idarucizumab safe and efficacious for the reversal of anticoagulation?

## Bottom Line

This prospective cohort study demonstrated that clotting tests normalised immediately following 2.5g of idarucizumab. Thrombotic events were recorded in 6% of patients following treatment. Further studies are required to assess the safety of idarucizumab, ideally with less pharmaceutical company involvement. However, in cases of life-threatening bleeding following dabigatran, idarucizumab should be considered for reversing anticoagulation effects.
