## Summary
The document discusses the implementation of the Emergency Psychiatric Assessment, Treatment, and Healing (EmPATH) unit in a tertiary referral ED in Iowa, aimed at improving care for patients presenting with suicidal ideation or attempts. The study compared outcomes before and after the unit's establishment, showing a significant reduction in psychiatric admissions from 57.1% to 27.3% and a decrease in ED boarding time from 16 to 5 hours. The EmPATH unit, which integrates psychiatrists, nurses, and social workers, facilitates stabilization and reduces unnecessary inpatient admissions. The authors conclude that the EmPATH unit effectively reduces ED boarding and admissions, though its generalizability may vary based on local conditions. The case resolution suggests evaluating local data to identify and address specific bottlenecks in mental health crisis management.

## Case
You are in discussion with your emergency department (ED) manager about the number of patients boarding for hours to days, and you are both aware that many of these patients are attending with mental health crises. You wonder whether a model of care involving a specifically designed unit would improve their patient experience and ED boarding times.

## Background
Emergency department (ED) visits in the United States for mental health conditions increased by 44% from 2006 to 2014, highlighting a significant strain on emergency mental health care resources, with patients often experiencing prolonged waits for inpatient admission. Clinician gestalt has been found to be as accurate and efficient as specific tools, such as the Convergent Functional Information for Suicidality screening tool, in assessing suicidality. Additionally, patients with three or more ED visits for alcohol-related issues face a 1-year mortality risk exceeding 6%, underscoring the clinical significance of frequent alcohol-related ED attendances.

## Clinical Point
Does the implementation of a dedicated interdisciplinary unit for mental health patients presenting to an ED with suicidal ideation or a suicide attempt reduce inpatient admissions and ED boarding time?

## SGEM Bottom Line
The EmPATH unit has been helpful in this setting in Iowa but generalizability will depend on how similar other centers are to the one studied.

## Case Resolution & Clinical Application
You agree with your manager that you need to look more closely at your local data to work out what are the rate-limiting steps locally, and then to address them with your psychiatric and social work teams.
