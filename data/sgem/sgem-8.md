## Summary
The document discusses the evaluation of the ABCD2 score's effectiveness in predicting stroke risk following a transient ischemic attack (TIA) in emergency department settings. A prospective cohort study involving 2056 patients across eight Canadian emergency departments found that the ABCD2 score, despite being recommended by the American Heart Association, is not a reliable predictor of stroke within 7 or 90 days post-TIA. The study concluded that the score lacks specificity and should not be used for risk stratification in ED patients. Instead, clinical judgment and local resources should guide the management and diagnostic work-up of these patients. The findings emphasize the need for alternative strategies in assessing stroke risk after TIA.

## Case Scenario
68yo woman with unilateral weakness lasting 20 minutes. Blood pressure 165/95.

## Point
Does the ABCD2 score predict who will go on to have a stroke in the next 7 or 90 days?

## BEEM Bottom Line
ABCD2 score is not a reliable way to risk stratify patients presenting to the ED with TIAs on who will go on to have a stroke.
