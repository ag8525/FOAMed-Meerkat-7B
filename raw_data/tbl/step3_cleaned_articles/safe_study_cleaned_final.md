# SAFE: A Comparison of Albumin and Saline for Fluid Resuscitation in the Intensive Care Unit

## Clinical Question
Does fluid resuscitation with albumin, compared to saline, affect mortality for patients in the ICU?

## Design
- Randomised controlled trial
- Stratified by centre and trauma diagnosis
- Double-blinded

## Setting
- 16 ICUs in Australasia
- Study period: November 2001 to June 2003

## Population

**Inclusion Criteria:**
- Adult patients admitted to academic tertiary ICUs requiring fluid administration to maintain or increase intravascular volume

**Exclusion Criteria:**
- Post-operative cardiac surgery
- Liver transplantation
- Burns

**Enrollment:**
- 6997 patients randomised

## Intervention

**Albumin Group:**
- Resuscitation with 4% albumin

## Control

**Saline Group:**
- Resuscitation with 0.9% sodium chloride

## Outcomes

### Primary Outcome
- All-cause mortality at 28 days:
  - Albumin: 20.9%
  - Saline: 21.1%
  - Relative risk of death: 0.99 (95% CI: 0.91–1.09), p = 0.87

### Secondary Outcomes
- No difference in:
  - Length of ICU stay
  - Duration of mechanical ventilation
  - Renal replacement therapy

- **Mortality at 28 days in specific subgroups:**
  - Severe sepsis: Albumin 30.7% vs Saline 35.3% (RR 0.87, 95% CI: 0.74–1.02), p = 0.09
  - Trauma: Albumin 13.6% vs Saline 10% (RR 1.36, 95% CI: 0.99–1.86), p = 0.06
  - Brain injury (post-hoc): Albumin 24.5% vs Saline 15.1% (RR 1.62, 95% CI: 1.12–2.34), p = 0.009

## Authors’ Conclusions
Use of 4% albumin or 0.9% sodium chloride for fluid resuscitation results in similar outcomes at 28 days. However, 4% albumin should be avoided in patients with head injuries.

## Strengths and Weaknesses
- **Strengths**
  - Multi-centre, large-scale trial
  - Double-blinded with specifically designed fluid containers and administration sets
  - Robust defense of the short-term safety profile of 4% albumin
- **Weaknesses**
  - Minimal weaknesses; sub-group analyses were underpowered

## Bottom Line
Overall, there is no difference in outcomes when using 4% albumin compared to 0.9% sodium chloride for fluid resuscitation. However, 4% albumin should be avoided in patients with head injuries. Further studies, such as the ALBIOS study, are needed for patients with severe sepsis.
