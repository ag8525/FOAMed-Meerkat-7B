# Idarucizumab for Dabigatran Reversal

## Clinical Question
In patients taking dabigatran who present with serious bleeding or require urgent surgery/intervention, is idarucizumab safe and efficacious for the reversal of anticoagulation?

## Design
- Prospective cohort study
- Blinding of clinicians to coagulation tests determining primary outcome
- Non-randomised
- Follow-up for at least 1 month

## Setting
- 184 sites in 35 countries
- Study period: June 2014 to February 2015

## Population

**Inclusion Criteria:**
- Adult patients taking dabigatran
- Group A: Patients with overt, uncontrollable, or life-threatening bleeding requiring a reversal agent (n=51)
- Group B: Patients requiring urgent surgery or invasive procedures that could not be delayed for at least 8 hours and required normal haemostasis (n=39)

**Exclusion Criteria:**
- None stated

**Enrollment:**
- 90 patients

## Intervention
- 5g IV idarucizumab administered as two 50ml bolus infusions, no more than 15 minutes apart

## Control
- No control group

## Outcomes

### Primary Outcome
- Maximum percentage reversal assessed by dilute thrombin time: 100% (median), 95% CI: 100-100%
  - Determined in 68 patients; 22 had normal baseline dilute thrombin times
- Maximum percentage reversal assessed by ecarin-clotting time: 100% (median), 95% CI: 100-100%
  - Determined in 81 patients; 9 had normal baseline ecarin-clotting times

### Secondary Outcomes
- Clotting tests normalised in:
  - Dilute thrombin time: 98% in Group A, 93% in Group B
  - Ecarin-clotting time: 89% in Group A, 88% in Group B
- Level of unbound dabigatran at presentation (median):
  - Group A: 84 ng/ml (range 3-641)
  - Group B: 76 ng/ml (range 4-2880)
- Unbound dabigatran <20 ng/ml (level with little/no anticoagulant effect) in:
  - 89% post 1st vial of idarucizumab
  - 93% at 12 hours
  - 79% at 24 hours

**Clinical Outcomes:**
- Mortality: 4.5% vs. 25% (normal vs. elevated clotting times at presentation)
- Thrombotic events: 9.0% vs. 4.4%
- Causes of death within 96 hours included intracranial bleeding, septic shock, and others
- Thrombotic events occurred in 5 patients (1 within 72 hours, 4 after 72 hours)
- Median time to cessation of bleeding in Group A: 11.4 hours
- 36 of 39 patients in Group B underwent urgent procedures with normal operative haemostasis reported in 92%

## Authors’ Conclusions
Idarucizumab completely reversed the effects of dabigatran in the majority of patients.

## Strengths and Weaknesses
- **Strengths**
  - Prospective design
  - Blinding of clinicians to coagulation tests determining primary outcome
  - Multi-centre study
  - Relevant population for immediate reversal of dabigatran effects
- **Weaknesses**
  - Significant conflict of interest with pharmaceutical company involvement
  - No control group
  - Causes of mortality unclear for some patients
  - Small sample size

## Bottom Line
This prospective cohort study demonstrated that clotting tests normalised immediately following 2.5g of idarucizumab. Thrombotic events were recorded in 6% of patients following treatment. Further studies are required to assess the safety of idarucizumab, ideally with less pharmaceutical company involvement. However, in cases of life-threatening bleeding following dabigatran, idarucizumab should be considered for reversing anticoagulation effects.
