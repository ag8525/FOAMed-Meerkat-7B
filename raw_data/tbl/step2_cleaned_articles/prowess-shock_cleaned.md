# Drotrecogin Alfa (Activated) in Adults with Septic Shock (PROWESS-SHOCK)

## Clinical Question
In patients with septic shock, does Activated Protein C (drotrecogin alfa or DrotAA) compared with placebo reduce mortality?

## Design
- Randomised, multi-centre, double-blind, placebo-controlled trial
- 6-month follow-up period
- Powered at 80% with α significance level of 0.05 to detect a 7% absolute mortality difference (20% relative difference) from an expected baseline mortality rate of 35%
- Recruitment target increased to 1696 due to lower-than-expected mortality rate of 27.6%

## Setting
- 208 sites worldwide (Europe, North and South America, Australia, New Zealand, and India)
- Study period: March 2008 to August 2011

## Population

**Inclusion Criteria:**
- Age ≥18 years
- Infection requiring IV antibiotics
- ≥2 SIRS criteria
- Septic shock, defined as:
  - At least 30 mL/kg IV fluids
  - ≥1 vasopressor for ≥4 hours
  - Continuing vasopressor requirements at randomisation with goal SBP of 90 mmHg or MAP of 65 mmHg
  - Hypoperfusion in the prior 36 hours, defined as ≥1 of:
    - Metabolic acidosis (base deficit ≥5 mmol/L, bicarbonate <18 mmol/L, lactate >2.5 mmol/L)
    - Urine output <0.5 mL/kg/h for an hour or a 50% increase in creatinine from baseline
    - Acute hepatic dysfunction (AST or ALT >500 IU/dL or bilirubin >2 g/dL)

**Exclusion Criteria:**
- Coexisting illnesses with high risk of death (e.g., metastatic cancer)

**Enrollment:**
- 27,816 potential patients screened, 1697 recruited and randomised
- Mean APACHE II scores: 25.2 (DrotAA group) and 25.5 (placebo group)

## Intervention
- DrotAA 24 mcg/kg of body weight per hour for 96 hours
- Mean duration administered: 83.3 hours
- Interrupted for procedures in 36.7%
- Study drug stopped early in 25.9%

## Control
- Placebo dissolved in 0.9% saline solution at equivalent infusion rate
- Mean duration administered: 85.1 hours
- Interrupted for procedures in 34.4%
- Study drug stopped early in 22.9%

## Outcomes

### Primary Outcome
- 28-day mortality: 26.4% in intervention vs 24.2% in control
  - Relative risk: 1.09 (95% CI: 0.92–1.28; p = 0.31)
  - Absolute risk reduction: -2.2% (95% CI: -2.0 to 6.3; NNH = 47)

### Secondary Outcomes
- 90-day mortality: 34.1% in intervention vs 32.7% in control
  - Relative risk: 1.04 (95% CI: 0.90–1.19; p = 0.56)
  - Absolute risk reduction: -1.36% (95% CI: -3.17 to 5.89; NNH = 74)
- SOFA score at day 7: No difference in cardiovascular, respiratory, renal, coagulation, or liver function

**Adverse Events:**
- Serious events by day 28: 14.3% in intervention vs 11.5% in control
  - Relative risk: 1.23 (95% CI: 0.96–1.59; p = 0.11)
  - Absolute risk reduction: -2.76% (95% CI: -0.46 to 5.98; NNH = 37)
- Non-serious bleeding events: 8.6% in intervention vs 4.8% in control
  - Relative risk: 1.80 (95% CI: 1.23–2.61; p = 0.002)
  - Absolute risk reduction: -3.84% (95% CI: 1.44 to 6.24; NNH = 27)
- Serious bleeding events: 1.2% in intervention vs 1.0% in control
  - Relative risk: 1.25 (95% CI: 0.49–3.15; p = 0.81)
  - Absolute risk reduction: -0.24% (95% CI: -0.75 to 1.23; NNH = 417)
- Cerebral haemorrhage events by day 28: 0.4% in both groups
  - Relative risk: 1.00 (95% CI: 0.20–4.90; p = 1.00)

## Authors’ Conclusions
DrotAA did not significantly reduce mortality at 28 or 90 days compared with placebo in patients with septic shock.

## Strengths and Weaknesses
- **Strengths**
  - Randomised, blinded, placebo-controlled
  - Targeted the sickest patients identified by PROWESS as most likely to benefit
  - Adaptive trial design maintained statistical power by increasing recruitment numbers when mortality was <30%
- **Weaknesses**
  - Funded by manufacturer
  - Low recruitment rate for severe sepsis is unexplained

## Bottom Line
DrotAA does not reduce mortality in patients with septic shock and is no longer available as the drug has been withdrawn.
