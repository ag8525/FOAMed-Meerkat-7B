# Thrombolysis Guided by Perfusion Imaging up to 9 Hours after Onset of Stroke (EXTEND Trial)

## Clinical Question
In patients with acute ischaemic stroke and salvageable brain tissue, does alteplase compared with placebo, administered between 4.5–9 hours post-stroke onset, improve functional outcome at 90 days?

## Background
- Intravenous thrombolysis for acute ischaemic stroke is typically limited to within 4.5 hours of onset.
- Perfusion imaging (CT/MRI) can identify viable brain tissue beyond 4.5 hours.
- Endovascular therapy has shown benefits up to 24 hours post-stroke in patients with salvageable tissue.

## Design
- Randomised controlled trial
- Double-blinded
- Placebo-controlled
- Multi-centre
- Intention-to-treat analysis
- Terminated early due to loss of equipoise following the WAKE-UP trial publication

## Setting
- 17 centres in Australasia, 10 in Taiwan, 1 in Finland
- Data collection: 2010–2018

## Population

**Inclusion Criteria:**
- Adults with excellent baseline function (mRS < 2)
- 4.5–9 hours post-stroke onset with NIHSS score of 4–26
- Hypoperfused but salvageable brain regions on CT/MRI perfusion imaging

**Exclusion Criteria:**
- Intracranial haemorrhage
- Rapidly improving symptoms
- Infarct core >1/3 MCA territory
- Recent stroke (within 3 months)
- Current anticoagulant use
- BP >185/110 or requiring aggressive treatment
- Consideration for endovascular thrombectomy

**Enrollment:**
- 225 patients randomised

## Intervention

**Alteplase Group:**
- Alteplase 0.9 mg/kg (max 90 mg) as a 10% bolus and 90% infusion over 1 hour

## Control

**Placebo Group:**
- Matching placebo

## Outcomes

### Primary Outcome
- **Excellent functional outcome at 90 days (mRS 0-1):**
  - Alteplase: 35.4%
  - Placebo: 29.5%
  - Adjusted risk ratio: 1.44 (95% CI: 1.01–2.06), p = 0.04
  - Unadjusted risk ratio: 1.2 (95% CI: 0.82–1.76), p = 0.35

### Secondary Outcomes
- **Functional independence at 90 days (mRS 0-2):**
  - Alteplase: 49.6%
  - Placebo: 42.9%
  - Adjusted OR: 1.36 (95% CI: 1.06–1.76)

- **Reperfusion at 24 hours ≥90%:**
  - Alteplase: 50%
  - Placebo: 28%
  - Adjusted OR: 1.73 (95% CI: 1.22–2.46)

- **Major neurologic improvement at 24 hours:**
  - Alteplase: 23.9%
  - Placebo: 9.8%
  - Adjusted OR: 2.76 (95% CI: 1.45–5.26)

### Safety Outcomes
- **Death within 90 days:**
  - Alteplase: 11.5%
  - Placebo: 8.9%
  - Adjusted OR: 1.17 (95% CI: 0.57–2.4)

- **Symptomatic intracranial haemorrhage within 36 hours:**
  - Alteplase: 6.2%
  - Placebo: 0.9%
  - Adjusted OR: 7.22 (95% CI: 0.97–2.4)

## Authors’ Conclusions
Alteplase therapy in patients with a favourable perfusion-imaging profile between 4.5–9 hours post-stroke onset or on awakening with stroke symptoms resulted in no or minor neurologic deficits more often than placebo. Further trials are needed due to study limitations.

## Strengths and Weaknesses
- **Strengths**
  - Randomised controlled trial
  - Double-blinded
  - Multi-centre
  - Intention-to-treat analysis
  - No loss to follow-up

- **Weaknesses**
  - Early termination of the study
  - Change in statistical analysis plan
  - Imbalances in baseline characteristics
  - Unadjusted outcomes did not show a benefit
  - Fragility index of 0
  - Uncertain time of symptom onset for patients waking with stroke symptoms

## Bottom Line
This trial reported a significant improvement in functional outcomes at 90 days for patients with salvageable brain tissue treated with alteplase 4.5–9 hours post-stroke onset compared to placebo. However, due to early termination and statistical analysis changes, further trials are required before recommending this treatment.
