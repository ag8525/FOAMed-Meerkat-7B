## Summary

The document discusses a phase 3, randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled study evaluating the efficacy of andexanet alfa in reversing the anticoagulation effects of apixaban and rivaroxaban in healthy adults. The study, conducted at two sites in the USA, involved 145 participants aged 50-75 years and demonstrated that andexanet significantly reduced anti-factor Xa activity and restored thrombin generation compared to placebo. The intervention included a bolus dose followed by an infusion, with results showing a marked reduction in anti-factor Xa activity and increased thrombin generation. No serious adverse events were reported, although one participant developed hives. The study highlights the potential of andexanet as a rapid reversal agent for factor Xa inhibitors, though further safety data from larger studies are recommended.

## Clinical Point

In healthy adults taking either apixaban (ANNEXA-A) or rivaroxaban (ANNEXA-R), does andexanet alfa compared to placebo normalize anticoagulation?

## Bottom Line

ANNEXA-A and ANNEXA-R demonstrate impressive reversal of anti-factor Xa activity by andexanet. Safety data from larger phase 3b-4 studies are needed. Caution is advised when using new drugs for emergency reversal of anticoagulation.
