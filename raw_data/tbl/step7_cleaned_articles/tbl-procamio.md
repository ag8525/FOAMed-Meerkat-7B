## Summary

The PROCAMIO trial compared intravenous procainamide and amiodarone for treating hemodynamically stable wide QRS tachycardia, likely ventricular in origin. Conducted across 26 Spanish hospitals, the study found that procainamide significantly reduced major adverse cardiac events (MACE) compared to amiodarone (9% vs. 41%, OR: "0.1, p = 0.006) and had a higher rate of tachycardia termination (67% vs. 38%, OR: 3.3, p = 0.026). Despite the trial's limitations, including its unblinded design and not reaching the estimated sample size, the findings suggest that procainamide may offer a safer and more effective alternative to amiodarone for this condition. However, the high adverse event rate in the amiodarone group and the limited availability of procainamide in some regions are noted concerns."

## Clinical Point

In patients with wide QRS (probably ventricular) tachycardia, does intravenous procainamide compared with intravenous amiodarone reduce major adverse cardiac events?

## Bottom Line

The trial suggests an improved safety profile and efficacy of 10 mg/kg intravenous procainamide over 20 minutes compared to 5 mg/kg intravenous amiodarone over 20 minutes in tolerated wide complex tachycardia.
