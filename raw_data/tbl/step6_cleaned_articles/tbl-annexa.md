# Andexanet Alfa for the Reversal of Factor Xa Inhibitor Activity

## Clinical Point
In healthy adults taking either apixaban (ANNEXA-A) or rivaroxaban (ANNEXA-R), does andexanet alfa compared to placebo normalize anticoagulation?

## Design
- Phase 3, randomised, double-blind, placebo-controlled study
- Central randomisation via web-based response system
- Two-part study within each sub-trial:
  - Part 1: Bolus dose of andexanet vs. placebo
  - Part 2: Bolus dose followed by a 2-hour infusion vs. placebo
- Double-blinded: participants and researchers unaware of allocation; only a designated research pharmacist aware
- 145 participants provided 99% power to detect a difference in anti-factor Xa activity change

## Setting
- Two sites:
  - ANNEXA-A: Arizona, USA
  - ANNEXA-R: California, USA
- March 2014 to May 2015

## Population

**Inclusion Criteria:**
- Healthy volunteers aged 50–75 years
- ANNEXA-A: 5 mg apixaban twice daily
- ANNEXA-R: 20 mg rivaroxaban once daily

**Exclusion Criteria:**
- History of abnormal bleeding, active bleeding, or risk factors for bleeding
- History of thrombosis or risk factors for thrombosis
- History of adult asthma or use of inhaled medications

**Enrollment:**
- 145 patients randomised to andexanet or placebo (3:1 ratio for ANNEXA-A, 2:1 for ANNEXA-R)

## Intervention

**ANNEXA-A:**
- Apixaban 5 mg orally twice daily for 3.5 days
- Andexanet administered 3 hours after last dose
  - Part 1: 400 mg andexanet IV bolus at 30 mg/min
  - Part 2: 400 mg andexanet IV bolus followed by 4 mg/min infusion for 120 minutes

**ANNEXA-R:**
- Rivaroxaban 20 mg orally once daily for 4 days
- Andexanet administered 4 hours after last dose
  - Part 1: 800 mg andexanet IV bolus at 30 mg/min
  - Part 2: 800 mg andexanet IV bolus followed by 8 mg/min infusion for 120 minutes

## Control
- Placebo matched to andexanet administration rates for all groups

## Outcomes

### Primary Outcome
- Anti-factor Xa activity reduced more with andexanet than placebo (mean reduction as % of baseline):
  - Part 1 (Bolus):
    - ANNEXA-A: 94±2% vs. 21±11% (p < 0.001)
    - ANNEXA-R: 92±11% vs. 21±15% (p < 0.001)
  - Part 2 (Bolus and Infusion):
    - ANNEXA-A: 92±3% vs. 33±6% (p < 0.001)
    - ANNEXA-R: 97±3% vs. 45±12% (p < 0.001)

### Secondary Outcome
- Thrombin generation restored with andexanet (change in mean thrombin generation):
  - Part 1 (Bolus):
    - ANNEXA-A: 1323.2±335.4 nM·min vs. 88.2±125.8 nM·min (p < 0.001)
    - ANNEXA-R: 1314.2±331.2 nM·min vs. 173.9±104.2 nM·min (p < 0.001)
  - Part 2 (Bolus and Infusion):
    - ANNEXA-A: 1193.1±263.3 nM·min vs. 189.4±184.8 nM·min (p < 0.001)
    - ANNEXA-R: 1510.4±344.8 nM·min vs. 264.4±140.7 nM·min (p < 0.001)

### Safety
- No serious or thrombotic events reported
- One patient developed hives, infusion discontinued after 35 minutes

## Authors’ Conclusions
Andexanet rapidly restores factor Xa activity, thrombin generation, and reduces unbound fraction of Xa inhibitors in older subjects treated with apixaban or rivaroxaban.

## Strengths and Weaknesses
- **Strengths**
  - Relevant clinical Point
  - Well-designed phase 3 study
  - Clear, objective outcomes
  - Appropriate blinding and study design
  - Inclusion of older subjects likely to take Xa inhibitors
- **Weaknesses**
  - Median age (53–60) lower than typical atrial fibrillation trials (70–73)
  - Subjects had normal renal function and BMI <28, not representative of typical Xa inhibitor users

## Bottom Line
ANNEXA-A and ANNEXA-R demonstrate impressive reversal of anti-factor Xa activity by andexanet. Safety data from larger phase 3b-4 studies are needed. Caution is advised when using new drugs for emergency reversal of anticoagulation.
