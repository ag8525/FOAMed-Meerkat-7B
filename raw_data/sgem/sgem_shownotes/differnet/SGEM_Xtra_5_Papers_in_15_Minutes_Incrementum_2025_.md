**Date:** March 23, 2025

I was honoured to be invited to speak at [Incrementum 2025](https://incrementum2025.com) in Mercia, Spain. Thank you to Paco, Carmen, and the Incrementum team for putting on one of the best conferences ever. They asked me to talk about five important recent papers in 15 minutes. Usually, I do ten papers in ten minutes. However, This was an international audience, with many of the close to 600 attendees having English as their second language. Therefore, I slowed down and covered five papers in 15 minutes.
