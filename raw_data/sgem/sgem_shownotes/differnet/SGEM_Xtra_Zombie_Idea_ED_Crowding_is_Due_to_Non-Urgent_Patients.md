**Date:** April 1, 2025

This is another SGEM Xtra. I was asked to give a public lecture for the Gateway Centre of Excellence in Rural Health. The topic was the zombie idea that emergency department crowding is due to non-urgent patients. You can click on this [LINK](https://thesgem.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/04/ED-crowding-Zombie.pdf) and get a PDF copy of my slides. The YouTube video of the presentation with the Q&A session featuring rural physician Dr. Kay Dingwell is available at this [LINK](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=-4hZgEDBiZg).

I also created a song to summarize some of the important parts of this issue ([LINK](https://thesgem.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/04/Zombie-Idea_-ED-Crowding-is-due-to-non-urgent-patients.-.mp3)). Thank you to Ivey MBA classmate Shirley Ngo, who was a guest skeptic on the SGEM Xtra: If I Had A Million Dollars episode discussing physician finance. She showed me how to make a song using artificial intelligence.
