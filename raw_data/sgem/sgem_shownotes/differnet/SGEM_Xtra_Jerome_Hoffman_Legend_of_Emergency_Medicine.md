**Date:**June 5th, 2017 

**Guest Skeptic:**Dr. Jerome Hoffman. Associate Program Director, [Emergency Medicine & Acute Care](https://courses.ccme.org/course/ema), Professor Emeritus of Medicine and Emergency Medicine, University of California, Los Angeles.

**Legends of Emergency Medicine:** This is the sixth episode in the summer series of [SGEM Xtras](http://thesgem.com/the-sgem-xtra/) called Legends of Emergency Medicine (#LegendofEM). These episodes highlight some of the pioneers and leaders of our specialty.  I hope these episodes will give the next generation of emergency physicians access to some super stars in our field via social media.

The format of the show will be similar. We will start by finding out some a non-medical thing about the legend: What song they picked as their music for the episode and why?  Then we will tell the back story of how we first met. The more formal part of the episode asks the legend five questions. Listen to the podcast on [iTunes](https://itunes.apple.com/ca/podcast/skeptics-guide-to-emergency/id564247833) to hear their responses.

**Five Questions for Jerry:**

![Dr. Jerome Hoffman](https://i0.wp.com/thesgem.com/wp-content/uploads/2017/08/maxresdefault-150x150.jpg?resize=150%2C150)

Dr. Jerome Hoffman

1. What is the biggest positive change you have seen in emergency medicine over your career?
2. What is the biggest negative change you have seen in emergency medicine over your career?
3. What contribution to emergency medicine that you made are you most proud of so far?
4. What advice would you give to someone who is interested going into emergency medicine?
5. How do you picture emergency medicine looking in the next ten years?

**Theme Music**: Each legend was asked to pick theme music for their episode and explain why they picked that particular song. Jerry picked [Strange Fruit](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hIveGYLe_v0) by Sidney Bachet.
