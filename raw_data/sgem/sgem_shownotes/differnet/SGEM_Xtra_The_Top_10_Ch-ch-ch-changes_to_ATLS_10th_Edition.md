Dr. Parry briefly tells the story of [Dr. James K. Styner](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/James_K._Styner) plane crash in 1976 that inspired him to start what became the Advanced Trauma and Life Support ([ATLS](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Advanced_trauma_life_support)) program. The ALTS program has been taught in 86 countries around the world and under gone a number of updates over last four decades.

Here are the top 10 changes to [ATLS 10th Edition](http://bulletin.facs.org/2018/06/atls-10th-edition-offers-new-insights-into-managing-trauma-patients/) according to Dr. Parry. You can listen to the SGEM podcast on [iTunes](https://itunes.apple.com/ca/podcast/skeptics-guide-to-emergency/id564247833) to hear Neil expand on all the points listed below.

### Top 10 Changes to ATLS 10th Edition

---

### **#1 – No More Lectures**

#### All interactive case – based discussions

#### Students need to read manuals!

#### True adult education – no spoon feeding

### **#2 – New Skills Stations**

#### Case-based unfolding scenarios for [A,B,C,D,E’s](https://goo.gl/images/LX1v6B)

#### No longer [anatomy based](https://www.google.com/search?q=atls+10th+edition+Brain+trauma+foundation&client=safari&rls=en&source=lnms&tbm=isch&sa=X&ved=0ahUKEwisoNz1wovdAhUJ7p8KHW_5DmIQ_AUIDCgD&biw=1129&bih=719#imgrc=RvzLroFuUCJdHM:)

#### More student interaction

### **#3 – “New/Honed” Skills for Instructors**

#### Must engage students

#### Time management

#### Five step method for skills teaching

### **#4 – Shock**

#### One litre crystalloid and early administration of balanced blood products for [Class III-IV shock](https://goo.gl/images/QvUVyT)

#### Updated shock classification table to include blood products

#### Introduce concept of damage control resuscitation

### **#5 – Thoracic Trauma**

#### Needle decompression 4-5th intercostal space, anterior axillary line for adults

#### Smaller chest tubes for hemothorax

#### Early management of blunt aortic injury

### **#6 – Head Trauma**

#### Revised [Glasgow Coma Scale](https://goo.gl/images/YzxcPf)

#### Introduce [Brain Trauma Foundation](https://braintrauma.org/uploads/03/12/Guidelines_for_Management_of_Severe_TBI_4th_Edition.pdf) 4th Guidelines

#### Paediatric Emergency Care Applied Research (PECARN) CT head guideline for [<2 years](https://goo.gl/images/DU5A3D) of age and [>2 years](https://goo.gl/images/gDEcGW) of age

### **#7 – Spine Trauma**

#### Introduce [Canadian C-spine Rules](https://www.mdcalc.com/canadian-c-spine-rule) and [NEXUS Criteria](https://www.mdcalc.com/nexus-criteria-c-spine-imaging) for C-spine imaging

#### Use the American Spinal Injury Association ([ASIA](http://asia-spinalinjury.org)) International Standards for Neurological Classification of Spinal Cord Injury ([ISNCSCI](http://asia-spinalinjury.org/international-standards-neurological-classification-sci-isncsci-worksheet/)) worksheet

### **#8 – Thermal Injury**

#### Resuscitation for deep partial/full thickness burns > 20 % BSA is 2cc RL x body weight (kg) x % BSA in [adults](https://goo.gl/images/5aDhCF)

#### [Paediatric](https://goo.gl/images/5aDhCF) is 3cc x weight (kg) x % BSA

#### Titrate fluid to [urine output](https://goo.gl/images/5aDhCF)

**![](https://i0.wp.com/thesgem.com/wp-content/uploads/2018/08/Team-Work-150x150.jpg?resize=150%2C150)**

### **#9 – Emphasis on Team Approach**

#### At the end of each chapter there is a real emphasis on team approach to resuscitation.

#### There is no *“i”* in the word *“team”*

### **#10 – Emphasis on Recognition of Intimate Partner Violence and Elderly Maltreatment**

#### 80% of intimate partner violence is suffered by women with a prevalence of 341/100,000 population in [Canada](https://www150.statcan.gc.ca/n1/pub/85-002-x/2013001/article/11805/11805-3-eng.htm)

#### Most elderly maltreatment is perpetrated by a [family member](https://www150.statcan.gc.ca/n1/pub/85-002-x/2014001/article/14114/section04-eng.htm)
