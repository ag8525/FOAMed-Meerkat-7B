**Date:** August 22nd, 2015

SGEMers have become familiar with Paediatric Emergency Medicine Super Hero [Dr. Anthony Crocco](http://thesgem.com/faculty/anthony-crocco/). He has been on the SGEM many times discussing the best paediatric emergency care. Anthony is also well know for his YouTube videos. There are now three RANThonys: [Fever Phobia](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4rKelmZqchk), [Cough Medication](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xxZmId9I6vU) and [Pain Control](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=bDghbN7I_SM).

Anthony launched his own evidence based medicine website earlier this year called [SketchyEBM](http://www.sketchyebm.com). This blog is another summer [SGEM Xtra](http://thesgem.com/the-sgem-xtra/) that will highlight this great new #FOAMed resource.

[![five+fingers](https://i0.wp.com/thesgem.com/wp-content/uploads/2014/06/five%2Bfingers-150x150.jpg?resize=150%2C150)](http://thesgem.com/2014/06/sgem80-crash-2-classic-paper/fivefingers/)**Five Questions for SketchyEBM:**

1. Give me your elevator pitch for SketchyEBM?
2. Where did you get the idea for SketchyEBM?
3. What are your goals for SketchyEBM?
4. What content will people find on SketchyEBM?
5. What other topics will you be covering on SketchyEBM?

If you are a program director, you need to check out SketchyEBM. If you are a student wanting to learn various EBM concepts, you need to check this website out. If you are running a Journal Club, you need to check this website out. Anyone interested in cutting the KT window down from over ten years to less than one year should check out SketchyEBM.

**Skeptics’ Guide to Emergency Medicine Season#4:**

Season#4 of the SGEM will be starting very soon. However, we need your help in selecting the articles to be reviewed. Please click on [Hot or Not](http://thesgem.com/hot-or-not/) to vote.
