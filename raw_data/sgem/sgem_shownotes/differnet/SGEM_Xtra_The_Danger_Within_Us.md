This is a [SGEM Xtra](http://thesgem.com/the-sgem-xtra/) book review. I had the pleasure of interviewing [Jeanne Lenzer](https://www.jeannelenzer.com). She is an award-winning independent medical investigative journalist who has written for the [BMJ](http://www.bmj.com). Jeanne has also written for the [Atlantic](https://www.theatlantic.com), the New York Times Magazine, [Mother Jones](https://www.motherjones.com), [Huffington Post](https://www.huffingtonpost.ca) and [Slate](https://slate.com).

Jeanne book is called [The Danger Within Us](https://www.amazon.com/Danger-Within-Us-Americas-Unregulated/dp/0316343765): America’s Untested, Unregulated Medical Device Industry and One Man’s Battle to Survive It. In the book, she takes a critical look at the medical device industry.

One of the main inspiration for her book is the Legend of Emergency Medicine [Dr. Jerome Hoffman](http://thesgem.com/2017/08/sgem-xtra-jerome-hoffman-legend-of-emergency-medicine/). He is also one of my mentors who taught me to be skeptical of the medical literature.

![Jeanne Lenzer](https://i0.wp.com/thesgem.com/wp-content/uploads/2018/04/DY5VpVLX0AUWOr8-150x150.png?resize=150%2C150)

Jeanne Lenzer

Listen to the podcast on [iTunes](https://itunes.apple.com/ca/podcast/skeptics-guide-to-emergency/id564247833) as Jeanne describes some of the problems with the medical device industry. Some of the disturbing facts she provides include:

* Medical interventions have become the third leading cause of death in America.
* The FDA does not require clinical testing for most high-risk implanted devices.
* The death rate caused by medical devices is unknown, because no one is keeping track.

Jeanne provides some of examples of patients who experienced significant harm instead of benefit with implantable medical devices like a vagus nerve stimulator or metal on metal artificial hip. We also discuss some of the possible solutions to the medical device industry and mention some additional resources: [Choosing Wisely](http://www.choosingwisely.org), [Lown Institute](http://lowninstitute.org), [Right Care Alliance](https://rightcarealliance.org), [ClinicalTrials.gov](http://ClinicalTrials.gov), [John Ioannidis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/John_Ioannidis), and [James McCormick](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=XXMcDtOO7NI).
