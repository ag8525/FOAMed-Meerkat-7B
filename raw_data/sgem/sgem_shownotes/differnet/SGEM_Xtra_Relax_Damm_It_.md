This is a [SGEM Xtra](http://thesgem.com/the-sgem-xtra/) book review. I had the pleasure of interviewing Professor [Timothy Caulfield](https://twitter.com/CaulfieldTim?ref_src=twsrc%5Egoogle%7Ctwcamp%5Eserp%7Ctwgr%5Eauthor). Tim is a Canadian professor of law at the University of Alberta, the Research Director of its Health Law Institute, and current Canada Research Chair in Health Law and Policy. His area of expertise is in legal, policy and ethical issues in medical research and its commercialization.

Tim came on the SGEM and discussed his new book called *[Relax, Dammit!](https://www.penguinrandomhouse.ca/books/588525/relax-dammit-by-timothy-caulfield/9780735236325) A User’s Guide to the Age of Anxiety*. Listen to the [podcast](https://podcasts.apple.com/ca/podcast/the-skeptics-guide-to-emergency-medicine/id564247833) to hear us discuss his new book, skepticism, and science communication in general.

![](https://i0.wp.com/thesgem.com/wp-content/uploads/2020/12/your-day-your-way-1-150x150.jpg?resize=150%2C150)The SGEM has a global audience with close to 45,000 subscribers. Many of the SGEMers live in the US and Tim’s book has a different title in America. It is called *[Your Day Your Way](https://www.amazon.ca/Your-Day-Way-Fiction-Decisions/dp/0762472499): The Facts and Fictions Behind Your Daily Decisions.* Tim gives some insight on the podcast why there is a different title in Canada and the US.

Tim and I met in 2015 at the Canadian Associate of Emergency Physicians ([CAEP](https://caep.ca)) Annual Conference in Edmonton. He was a keynote speaker and discussed his previous book *[Is Gwyneth Paltrow Wrong about Everything?](https://www.amazon.ca/Gwyneth-Paltrow-Wrong-About-Everything/dp/0807039705) How the Famous Sell Us Elixirs of Health, Beauty & Happiness*. Tim gave a fantastic presentation. I was in Edmonton talking nerdy as part of the CAEP TV initiative. We have been in contact via social media ever since trying to improve science communication.

![](https://i0.wp.com/thesgem.com/wp-content/uploads/2020/12/Ken-and-Tim-CAEP-2015-rotated.jpg?resize=1080%2C810)

Besides writing books, Tim has stared in his own Netflix series called: *[A User guide to Cheating Death](https://www.imdb.com/title/tt7532396/).* He has also collaborated Dr. Jennifer Gunter who wrote the book *[The Vagina Bible](https://www.amazon.ca/Vagina-Bible-vulva-vagina-separating-medicine/dp/0735277370)*. Dr. Gunter visited BatDoc a few years ago for an [SGEM Xtra](https://thesgem.com/2017/10/sgem-xtra-dr-jennifer-gunter-super-hero-of-science/) extra episode.
