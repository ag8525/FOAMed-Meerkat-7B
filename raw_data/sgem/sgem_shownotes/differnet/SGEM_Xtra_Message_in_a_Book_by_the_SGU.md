**Date:** December 4th, 2018

**Guest Skeptic:** Dr. Steven Novella is a Yale Neurologist, host and producer of the popular science podcast called the Skeptics Guide to the Universe and executive editor of [Science Based Medicine](https://sciencebasedmedicine.org) blog.

This is an [SGEM Xtra](http://thesgem.com/the-sgem-xtra/) book review. I had the privilege of interviewing Dr. Novella about the new book by the [Skeptics’ Guide to the Universe](https://www.theskepticsguide.org) (SGU).  I would like to recognize the influence the SGU had on inspiring the SGEM. The SGU has been a big part of my skeptical journey and contributed greatly to my critical thinking skills.

Listen to the SGEM podcast on [iTunes](https://itunes.apple.com/ca/podcast/skeptics-guide-to-emergency/id564247833) or your favourite podcast app hear Dr. Novella and me discuss how SGU got started, their new book, how to encourage critical thinking with students and colleagues, how he discusses pseudoscience with colleagues and patients and a few other topics.

**Five Frivolous Questions for Dr. Novella:**

2. Best Captain, Picard or Kirk?
3. If you could have one today, flying car or transporter?
4. Dinner with Newton or Galileo?
5. Would you rather visit Mars or a moon of Jupiter?
