**Date:**  June 21st, 2017

**Guest Skeptic:**Dr. Joseph Lex is considered by many to be the Godfather of the [FOAM](http://lifeinthefastlane.com/foam/) (Free Open Access to Medication) movement. Joe is a retired Professor of Emergency Medicine from [Temple University](http://www.temple.edu/medicine/faculty/l/lex.asp?pms=(lex+JR%5Bau)). He created a site called [Free Emergency Medicine Talks](http://freeemergencytalks.net/) that has thousands of recorded of lectures. It is an unbelievable global and free resource for anyone interested in emergency medicine.

**Legends of Emergency Medicine:** This is the third episode in the summer series of [SGEM Xtras](http://thesgem.com/the-sgem-xtra/) called Legends of Emergency Medicine. These episodes highlight some of the pioneers and leaders of our specialty.  I hope these episodes will give the next generation of emergency physicians access to some super stars in our field via social media.

The format of the show will be similar. We will start by finding out some a non-medical thing about the legend: What song they picked as their music for the episode and why?  Then we will tell the back story of how we first met. The more formal part of the episode asks the legend five questions. Listen to the podcast on [iTunes](https://itunes.apple.com/ca/podcast/skeptics-guide-to-emergency/id564247833) to hear their responses.

Dr. Joe Lex (Godfather of #FOAMed)

**Five Questions for Joe:**

1. What is the biggest positive change you have seen in emergency medicine over your career?
2. What is the biggest negative change you have seen in emergency medicine over your career?
3. What contribution to emergency medicine that you made are you most proud of so far?
4. What advice would you give to someone who is interested going into emergency medicine?
5. How do you picture emergency medicine looking in the next ten years?

**Theme Music**: Each legend was asked to pick theme music for their episode and explain why they picked that particular song. Joe picked [Done a lot of things Wrong](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9FkfAuwsre8) by Paul Butterfield.

**Legendary #FOAMed Statement:** Joe recorded his legendary statement about learning emergency medicine. For the standard version and for Joe’s impression of Marlon Brando version from the Godfather click [FOAM Godfather](http://thesgem.com/2014/04/the-godfather-of-foamed/).
