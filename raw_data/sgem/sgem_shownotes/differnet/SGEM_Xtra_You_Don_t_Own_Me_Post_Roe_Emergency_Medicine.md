### QUESTIONS FOR DR. MICHELLE LIN

---

Dr. Michelle Lin was asked several questions about the implications of the Dobbs v Jackson Women’s Health Organization decision. Please listen to the [SGEM Xtra](https://thesgem.com/the-sgem-xtra/) podcast on iTunes to hear her answers and for more details.

![](https://i0.wp.com/thesgem.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/01/Michelle-Lin.jpeg?resize=150%2C150&ssl=1)

Dr. Michelle Lin

1. Why does Emergency Medicine need to know? After all, we aren’t abortion providers, or even Obstetrician/Gynaecologists.
2. The paper talks about the unequal implications of this decision – surely the law is the law everywhere? Can you tell us more?
3. What are the legal implications for Emergency Physicians caring for patients who may be miscarrying spontaneously, have an ectopic pregnancy, or present with the complications of an abortion which is illegal in that state?
4. You said something specifically about documentation as well?
5. And what about the pregnant or potentially pregnant Emergency Physician? Is this going to impact on us as a workforce too?
6. You’ve mentioned that one of the policy actions for emergency physicians could be to use [EMTALA](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Emergency_Medical_Treatment_and_Active_Labor_Act) to pre-empt state restrictions. Can you explain how that would work practically for those of us outside the US who don’t have an EMTALA-like system?
7. Is there anything else you would like to say about this issue?
