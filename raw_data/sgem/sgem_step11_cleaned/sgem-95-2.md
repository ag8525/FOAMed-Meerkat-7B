**Summary:**
The document discusses the management of fever in children, particularly addressing parental concerns about febrile seizures. It highlights that fever itself is not harmful to generally healthy children and may even be beneficial. A systematic review and meta-analysis by Rosenbloom et al. evaluated the effectiveness of antipyretics in preventing the recurrence of febrile seizures, concluding that antipyretics do not significantly reduce seizure recurrence rates, with an odds ratio of 0.9 (95% CI: 0.57-1.43). The study faced limitations, including lack of grey literature search and potential bias risks. Clinically, antipyretics should not be relied upon to prevent febrile seizures, and parents should be reassured that treating fever is primarily for comfort, not seizure prevention.

**Case:**
2-year-old presents with flu-like symptoms and a fever.

**Background:**
Fever in children is a common concern among parents, often leading to unnecessary emergency department visits due to misconceptions about its dangers. According to the American Academy of Pediatrics, fever itself does not pose a risk to generally healthy children and may even be beneficial. The primary objective of antipyretic treatment is not to reduce body temperature to normal levels but to enhance the child's comfort and overall well-being.

**Point:**
Will treating with antipyretics prevent a febrile seizure?

**SGEM Bottom Line:**
Antipyretics appear to offer no significant improvement in the recurrence rates of febrile seizures in children.

**Clinical Application:**
In children with febrile seizures, the regular use of antipyretics appears to have no significant effect on reducing the rates of seizure recurrence.

**What Do I Tell My Patient?**
Treating your child’s fever will not likely have any effect on recurrence rates of febrile seizures.

**Summary:**
- Antipyretics don’t appear to lengthen duration of fever in ill children
- Antipyretics can be combined for effect, but to what end?
- Antipyretics don’t appear to decrease risk of febrile seizure recurrence
- Fever: Not your enemy!
