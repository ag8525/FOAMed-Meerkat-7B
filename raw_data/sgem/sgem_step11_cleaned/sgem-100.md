**Summary:**
The document humorously explores the concept of applying evidence-based medicine principles to dating, specifically for medical professionals who may struggle with relationships due to their demanding careers. It introduces the Early Goal Directed Dating (EGDD) protocol, a playful analogy to Early Goal Directed Therapy in sepsis management, aimed at improving relationship outcomes such as marriage or cohabitation. The study, though speculative and limited by its retrospective design, suggests that early application of the EGDD algorithm may provide clarity in romantic relationships. The authors acknowledge the need for a prospective study to validate their claims, while emphasizing the importance of appropriate study design and skepticism towards evidence, even in parody studies. The narrative concludes with a fictional case resolution where the protagonist successfully applies the EGDD algorithm, highlighting the importance of communication in relationships.

**Case:**
Jason is a fellow in Cardiology, despite knowing everything about the heart he finds himself struggling with his own. He can easily recite the path of the electrical current from the SA node, through the atria, to the AV node, through the bundle of HIS and Purkinje fibers but he becomes easily lost when required to navigate the path of a relationship. He has recently been on a couple of dates with a person of interest but doesn’t know what the next step is.

**Background:**
The text discusses the challenges young physicians face in maintaining personal relationships due to demanding work schedules, highlighting the concept of evidence-based dating as a humorous yet insightful approach to relationship management. The Johnson-Purdy nomogram, inspired by the Fagan nomogram used in medical diagnostics, and the Canadian ITAD Decision Tool are introduced as methods to assess the likelihood of being on a date. Additionally, the text references the ProCESS and ARISE trials, which concluded that Early Goal Directed Therapy (EGDT) for sepsis is not superior to usual care, emphasizing the importance of early sepsis recognition and treatment. A new protocol, Early Goal Directed Dating (EGDD), is proposed to aid physicians in improving relationship outcomes, developed through qualitative methods and validated against physicians' relationship experiences.

**Clinical Point:**
What are the key components/milestones that increase the likelihood of marriage/co-habitation?

**Case Resolution:**
Jason, being up to date on the evidence-based dating literature (Johnson-Purdy nomogram and Canadian ITAD Decision Tool) and decided to apply the EGDD algorithm. He realized that the next best step was to communicate clearly with the person of interest about the status of their relationship. Together they decided that they would change their Facebook statuses and they have been progressing down the EGDD algorithm since. The authors expect an invite to their wedding.

**What Do I Tell My Friend?**
Don’t stress and have fun! Remember that all the steps are part of the journey.
