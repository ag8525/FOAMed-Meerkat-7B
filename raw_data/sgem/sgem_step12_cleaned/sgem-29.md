## Summary
The document discusses the IST-3 trial, which evaluated the efficacy and safety of thrombolysis with tPA in acute ischemic stroke patients treated within 6 hours of onset, focusing on those over 80 years old. The trial, involving 3035 patients, found no significant difference in the primary outcome of being alive and independent at six months between the tPA and placebo groups (37% vs 35%, adjusted OR 1.13). However, there was a higher risk of early death and intracerebral hemorrhage in the tPA group. Despite these risks, the authors concluded that thrombolysis improved functional outcomes, although the trial faced several limitations, including not meeting its target sample size and issues with trial design and reporting. The BEEM comments highlight the increased harm and lack of significant benefit, leading to a shared decision against tPA use in the presented case.

## Case Scenario
An 83-year-old woman arrives from home with right-sided weakness beginning four hours prior. You diagnose acute ischemic CVA with no contraindications to thrombolysis.

## Point
Does thrombolysis given to acute ischemic CVA in <6hrs increase the proportion of people who are alive and independent at six months?

## Background
Acute ischemic strokes are a leading cause of disability and the third most common cause of death. Thrombolysis, particularly with tissue plasminogen activator (tPA), is a standard treatment approach, initially approved in the European Union for use within 3 hours of stroke onset and in patients under 80 years old. However, a Cochrane review suggested potential benefits of tPA administration up to 6 hours post-stroke. The IST-3 study aimed to evaluate the risks and benefits of tPA in patients not meeting the original licensing criteria, focusing on older patients and extending the treatment window to 6 hours.

## BEEM Bottom Line
Treatment with tPA in this study harmed (death) 1 in 25 early, the fatal and non-fatal bleed rate went up significantly and there was no benefit seen at six months in the primary outcome.

## Case Resolution
You discuss the options with the patient and their family. Given her age and the time now being over 4.5hrs, you reach a shared decision not to use tPA.
