## Summary
The GRACE-3 guidelines focus on the evaluation and management of adult emergency department (ED) patients presenting with acute dizziness or vertigo, specifically those without obvious central or general medical causes. The guidelines emphasize the importance of training emergency clinicians in bedside examination techniques, such as the HINTS exam and maneuvers for diagnosing and treating BPPV, to improve diagnostic accuracy. Key recommendations include using the HINTS exam for patients with acute vestibular syndrome (AVS) and nystagmus to differentiate between central and peripheral causes, and against routine use of CT or MRI as first-line diagnostics unless HINTS results are central or equivocal. The guidelines also suggest shared decision-making for steroid use in vestibular neuritis and recommend the Epley maneuver for BPPV diagnosed by a positive Dix-Hallpike test. The document highlights the need for improved training and the use of a timing and triggers approach rather than relying solely on patient-reported symptoms.
